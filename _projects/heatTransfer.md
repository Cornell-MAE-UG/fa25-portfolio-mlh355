In my heat transfer class in the spring of 2026 I learned about the different modes of heat exchange and how they work together. One problem in particular that I felt like was very valuable was an analysis of a wire submerged in an oil bath. This wire produced Joule heating and such was being cooled by the oil bath. The problem first asked me to calculate the steady state temperature of the wire, then to derive an expression for the temperature as a function of time. This problem challenged me to utilize equilibrium equations, make assumptions, and explore the lumped capacitance method.
