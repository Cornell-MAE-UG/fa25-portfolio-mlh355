During this class I strengthed my circuit analysis skills, and using everything I learned completed a final project to create a robot powered by an ArduinoUno. The goal of the competition was to gather more blocks than your opponent. My groups robot was able to advance into the round of 16, if you'd like to read more check out our final report!
